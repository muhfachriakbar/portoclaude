Why Claude Campus Ambassador

I've already run the version of this job without the title. The Adami Google AI Tools Masterclass proved I can take a technical subject, build a curriculum around it, and get a multi-university audience to sit through 8 workshops and rate it a perfect 10/10. Claude asks for exactly that muscle: turning "how do I use an AI assistant well" into workshops, demos, and conversations students actually want to attend.

I'd rather build community than broadcast to it. Leading a 32-member committee, coordinating a 1,700-member scout delegation, and running a student council all taught me the same thing — adoption happens through people you've trained to carry the message further. That's how I'd approach Claude on campus: a core group of confident users first, then workshops and content that let them spread it themselves.

And I already have the distribution. A content channel built specifically around AI literacy, reaching 1M+ views a month, means campus outreach doesn't start from zero — it starts from an audience that already trusts me to explain this stuff clearly.

  • Train Design and run hands-on Claude workshops, using the same format that got a 10/10 satisfaction score at national scale.

  • Organize Recruit and lead a campus committee, the way I've led 32- and 55-member teams before, so the program outlives any single event.
