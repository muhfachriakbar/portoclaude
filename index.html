I'm currently studying Construction Services at Politeknik Negeri Ujung Pandang (PNUP) in Makassar, where coursework in construction management and technical problem-solving has taught me to think in systems — how a plan holds together under real-world constraints. I've carried that same systems mindset into how I use and teach AI, treating it as infrastructure for better research, learning, and decision-making rather than a novelty.

Why Claude Campus Ambassador

I've already run the version of this job without the title. The Adami Google AI Tools Masterclass proved I can take a technical subject, build a curriculum around it, and get a multi-university audience to sit through 8 workshops and rate it a perfect 10/10. Claude asks for exactly that muscle: turning "how do I use an AI assistant well" into workshops, demos, and conversations students actually want to attend.

I'd rather build community than broadcast to it. Leading a 32-member committee, coordinating a 1,700-member scout delegation, and running a student council all taught me the same thing — adoption happens through people you've trained to carry the message further, not through a single event. That's how I'd approach Claude on campus: a core group of confident users first, then workshops and content that let them spread it themselves.

And I already have the distribution. A content channel built specifically around AI literacy, reaching 1M+ views a month, means campus outreach doesn't start from zero — it starts from an audience that already trusts me to explain this stuff clearly.

  • TrainDesign and run hands-on Claude workshops, using the same format that got a 10/10 satisfaction score at national scale.
  • CreateProduce short-form content that teaches practical Claude use cases to a student audience already built and engaged.
  • OrganizeRecruit and lead a campus committee, the way I've led 32- and 55-member teams before, so the program outlives any single event.
  • BridgeConnect a civil engineering department with computing and AI communities across 9+ universities I already have ties to.
